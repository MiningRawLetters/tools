# MRL Space Twin (Simplified)

**Simple Space Center pairing for RoboFont**

## Features

- **Master & Twin Selection**: Choose any two Space Centers from the list
- **Easy Pairing**: Click "Pair :-)" to pair selected master and twin
- **Status Indicators**: Clear visual status for each Space Center
- **Active Pairs Management**: View and manage active pairs
- **Unpair Functionality**: Remove pairs when no longer needed
- **One-time Sync**: Initial text synchronization when pairing

## Usage

1. **Open two Space Centers** in RoboFont (any fonts)
2. **Launch MRL Space Twin** from Extensions menu
3. **Select a Space Center** from the list and click "Set Master"
4. **Select another Space Center** from the list and click "Set Twin"
5. **Click "Pair :-)"** to pair them together
6. The twin will sync the current text from the master
7. View active pairs in the bottom list
8. Select a pair and click "Unpair" to remove it

## Controls

- **Set Master**: Makes the selected Space Center the master
- **Set Twin**: Makes the selected Space Center the twin
- **Pair :-)**: Pairs the selected master and twin (includes smiley!)
- **Unpair**: Removes the selected pair
- **Close**: Closes the extension

## Status Indicators

- **MASTER**: Currently selected as master
- **TWIN**: Currently selected as twin
- **Paired**: Already part of an active pair
- **Available**: Ready to be selected

## Benefits

- Simple workflow for comparing fonts
- Clear visual feedback for selections
- Easy pair management
- Perfect for font comparison workflows
- Intuitive two-step selection process
